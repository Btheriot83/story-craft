# Sources & method
These sources inform craft and ethics. They do not verify the facts of a case. Case evidence belongs in a separate, locatable claim ledger. Editorial timing targets and scoring thresholds are Story Craft policy, not claims made by these authors.

## Accuracy and human impact
- [National Center for Victims of Crime — Creating Survivor-Centered Media (2025)](https://victimsofcrime.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/NCVC-MEDIA-GUIDE_GUIDE-FOR-CONTENT-PRODUCERS-1-1.pdf): use accurate, survivor-centered framing; prioritize primary sources and human impact; avoid invented thoughts, speculation and offender glamorization.
- [Society of Professional Journalists — Code of Ethics](https://www.spj.org/spj-code-of-ethics/): verify, use original sources, preserve context, attribute, seek response to allegations, minimize harm and identify reenactments. The code is ethical guidance, not a case adjudication.

## Scene and sequence
- [Nieman Storyboard — Six tips for crafting scenes](https://niemanstoryboard.org/2005/03/24/six-tips-for-crafting-scenes/): action, movement, tension and telling detail help scenes do narrative work. In factual work, details still require reporting.
- [Nieman Storyboard — Organizing nonfiction scenes](https://niemanstoryboard.org/2024/07/24/nonfiction-journalism-books-structure-organizing-scenes/): identify defining moments, preserve the causal chain, build a master timeline, and orient readers when time shifts.

## Writing for the ear
- [Ira Glass — Transom](https://transom.org/2004/ira-glass/): action and reflection work together; tell stories aloud and look for human moments.
- [BBC Academy — Huw Edwards, Writing News](https://downloads.bbc.co.uk/academy/academyfiles/Huw_Edwards_Writing_News_Transcript_ENGLISH.pdf): write clearly, concisely and correctly; read aloud; know the main point and make the ending intentional.

## A useful genre boundary
- [Romance Writers of America — About Romance Fiction](https://www.rwa.org/about-romance-fiction): genre romance requires a central love story and an emotionally satisfying, optimistic ending. That distinguishes romance from tragic relationship-led crime; it does not impose a happy ending on factual events.

## Research hierarchy
Start with records closest to the claim, then use credible reporting and scholarship to establish context and identify disputes. Check provenance, corrections, date, translation and later developments. A transcript proves that someone said something, not automatically that it happened. Multiple articles repeating one source are not independent corroboration.
