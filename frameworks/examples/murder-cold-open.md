# Example — Murder cold open (ORIGINAL, annotated)

**Beats:** `murder.hook` + `murder.stake` seed  
**~210 words** — fragment only; full piece needs fair clues + reveal punch.

---

## Text

By the time Detective Ruiz reaches the third-floor walk-up, the kettle has already screamed itself dry. The apartment smells like scorched metal and orange peels. On the table: two cups. One lipstick-kissed at the rim. One untouched, still full, a fly practicing lazy circles on the surface.

The dead man sits upright on the couch as if waiting for the second sip. No blood. Just a quiet that feels arranged.

Ruiz’s trainee points at the window latch—open, though it’s February. “Suicide?” he offers, hopeful for simplicity.

Ruiz doesn’t answer. She lifts the full cup, sniffs. Bitter almonds, faint as a rumor. Then she notices the peels in the sink: six oranges, pith scraped obsessively clean, as if someone needed the skins for something else—or needed the time it took.

On the fridge, a magnet holds a photo of the dead man and a woman mid-laugh on a pier. The woman’s smile is wide. The magnet is a tiny brass key.

Ruiz’s phone buzzes. The captain: *Victim’s sister is downstairs. She says he never drank tea.*

Ruiz looks back at the two cups. The lipstick. The fly. The open latch letting out a secret that didn’t want to stay warm.

“Not suicide,” she says. “Someone stayed for manners.”

## Agent lesson
Open plants multiple dual-reading details. Ending must **reassemble** these (not invent cyanide-out-of-nowhere without the almond sniff already here). Stopping at atmosphere = FAIL.
