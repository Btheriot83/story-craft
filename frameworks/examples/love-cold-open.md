# Example — Love cold open (ORIGINAL, annotated)

**Target use:** Beginning beats `love.hook` + start of `love.stake`  
**Word count:** ~220  
**Note:** Illustrative fragment, not a full ≤5-min script. Ending beats omitted on purpose — full scripts must still pass Strong Ending gate.

---

## Text

The fire door sticks halfway, which is how Mara ends up chest-to-chest with the man who ghosted her at the altar eighteen months ago—except he is holding a sheet cake that says **CONGRATULATIONS, JULIAN** in trembling blue gel, and the hallway smoke alarm is already screaming like it knows.

“Wrong floor,” Julian says, cake between them like a hostage.

“Wrong decade,” Mara says, and does not step back. Elevator’s dead; stairs behind her are a vertical oven. His building. Her catering shift. Someone else’s party. Heat crawls up the stairwell and paints his throat shiny. She can smell almond frosting and the cologne he wore the morning he didn’t arrive.

He adjusts his grip. A smear of blue gels onto her wrist, cold as a ring.

“They’re waiting,” he says.

“Then drop the cake and go be congratulated.”

Something flickers—shame, or hunger—and he doesn’t move. The alarm keeps promising the worst. Mara’s heartbeat answers it, traitorously on beat with his. She came here to deliver dessert and leave with cash. She did not come here to learn whether the man who ran can stand still when the exit is literally on fire.

Julian swallows. “Mara. If I put this down—”

“Don’t you dare make a speech with frosting on your hands.”

But she doesn’t take the stairs. Neither does he. The cake tilts. The blue letters smear into a single unreadable river—and for one held breath, congratulations belong to nobody.

## Beat labels
| Segment | Beat | Act phase | Notes |
|---------|------|-----------|-------|
| Fire door / cake / alarm | `love.hook` | beginning | Sensory cold open; question: will they face each other? |
| Ghosted at altar / wrong floor | `love.stake` (seed) | beginning | Wound + cost; shared trap = adhesion seed |
| Blue gel on wrist | plant | beginning | Token for ending echo (agents: later rhyme with commitment mark / smeared word made clear) |
| Neither takes the stairs | midpoint seed | middle-capable | Attraction under pressure; exit refused |

## Agent lesson
Cold open already *progresses*: trap tightens, wound surfaces, token planted. A full love script must still escalate to black moment and an **earned HEA/HFN punch**—this fragment alone would FAIL ending QA if stopped here.
