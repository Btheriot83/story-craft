# Example — Crime of passion cold open (ORIGINAL, annotated)

**Beats:** `passion.hook` + `passion.bond` seed  
**~230 words** — fragment; full script needs escalation, on-page act, aftermath punch.

---

## Text

The voicemail is only twelve seconds long, which is somehow worse.

*—don’t wait up, baby, the preview ran late, love you—*

Lena plays it again in the parking garage under the studio lot, forehead on the steering wheel, while the timestamp glows: 9:42 p.m. On the passenger seat, his leather jacket—left in her car “by accident”—still holds the shape of his shoulders. In the inside pocket she already found the other thing: a hotel key card folded into a receipt for two desserts.

She tells herself she will drive home. She tells herself she is a reasonable woman who edits other people’s love stories for a living and knows how these scenes end.

Instead she starts the engine and follows the route pinned in his shared maps, the little blue dot sliding toward the place that is not late preview, not work, not love-you-baby. Rain needles the windshield. She keeps the voicemail playing on loop until the words abrade into pure sound—soft, false, almost tender.

When she parks across from the hotel’s glass doors, she sees him through the lobby light with a woman whose laugh she recognizes from the office holiday party. His hand sits at the woman’s waist like it belongs there.

Lena opens the glove box. The tire iron is ugly and honest. She does not touch it yet. She touches the wedding band he swore was forever, and feels the metal go cold as if it, too, has heard the twelve-second lie.
