# Love — Craft at ≤5 Minutes (~600–750 words)

**Genre ID:** `genre-love`  
**Promise:** Two people move toward intimacy; end with earned HEA or HFN (not ambiguous fade).  
**Cast budget:** 2 leads + ≤1 catalyst. No subplot romance.

---

## What works at this length

Love at flash/short-read length is a **relationship transformation**, not a biography. Compress commercial romance’s Setup → Falling → Retreat → Fighting phases into ~6–8 beats (Jami Gold short-story marks; instalove five-move compression; Gwen Hayes-style dark moment → earned gesture).

One **wound/lie** per lead (or shared), one **adhesion** (forced proximity / shared problem), one **black moment** caused by that wound (not a fixable misunderstanding), one **costly choice** that proves change, then a **closing image** that answers the opening question.

---

## Beginning (0:00–~1:00 | first ~120–180 words)

### Goals
1. **Cold open:** Drop into a charged specific moment (Handley): desire, awkwardness, danger-to-the-heart — not “once they met.”
2. **Why we care in 30–60s:** Show a want + a cost if they fail (loneliness, pride, leaving town tomorrow, last chance).
3. **Stake setup:** What intimacy threatens (career, safety, identity, another loyalty).
4. **Promise:** Tone signals HEA/HFN-capable story (warmth + friction), not tragedy-as-default unless labeled otherwise.

### Hook patterns that progress
| Pattern | Then progress by… |
|---------|-------------------|
| Mid-argument / mid-almost-kiss | Reveal the *why* of the fight in the next beat |
| Meet under pressure (stuck elevator, shared crisis) | Show complementary competence + opposing values |
| One lead watches the other do something revealing | Force an interaction that risks exposure |
| Text line that contradicts behavior | Let subtext become text under pressure |

### Avoid
- Meet-cute then three paragraphs of biography.
- Instant perfect compatibility with no friction.
- “Warm open” reporting (“Last Tuesday I met…”).

---

## Middle (~1:00–~3:30 | escalation — no sag)

### Goals
- **Escalate intimacy and conflict together** (Yorke midpoint: meaning or stakes flip).
- **Character pressure:** Each attempt at connection triggers the wound.
- **Timeline clarity:** Prefer one continuous evening / one day / one journey. If flashback, one clear frame.

### Escalation ladder (pick 2–3, stack)
1. Forced proximity / shared task (adhesion).
2. Vulnerability exchange (one secret, one kindness).
3. Midpoint commitment or kiss that raises cost of walking away.
4. External clock (train, flight, wedding, eviction, sunrise).
5. Black-moment setup: wound resurfaces *because* they got close.

### Anti-sag rules (≤5 min)
- Every 20–30 spoken seconds: new information, choice, or status change between them.
- Cut third-wheel comedy, travel montages, “they dated for weeks” summaries.
- Dialogue: late in / early out (NYC Midnight).

### Character depth without backstory dumps
Reveal via **choice under pressure**, not essay. One concrete childhood/past detail max, attached to present action (multi-function detail — River/Supple).

---

## Ending (~3:30–5:00 | REQUIRED punch — non-negotiable)

Weak / fade-out / “maybe someday” without HFN commitment = **FAIL**.

### Ending must deliver
1. **Crisis:** Separation or near-loss rooted in the wound (Hayes dark moment).
2. **Climax choice:** At least one lead changes behavior on-page (grand gesture *specific* to the other’s need — not generic airport run).
3. **Payoff:** HEA (clear lasting commitment) or HFN (together + hopeful next step stated). Genre contract kept (Stanley / HEA–HFN craft).
4. **Resonance:** Echo opening image/line with new meaning (Kidder; Writers.com surprising-yet-inevitable).
5. **Short coda:** One beat of afterglow or future-glimpse — not epilogue sprawl (Writers in the Storm: keep resolution tight).

### Strong closing patterns (love)
| Pattern | How it punches |
|---------|----------------|
| **Mirrored cold open** | Same place/line as open; now they choose each other |
| **Specific gesture** | Action that only *this* wounded person would recognize as love |
| **Spoken truth after silence** | The withheld sentence finally said, then consequence |
| **Clock beaten together** | Shared win against the external timer |
| **HFN doorway** | Not “happily ever,” but “we’re going home / stay tonight / tell them tomorrow” with clear togetherness |

### Ending anti-patterns (auto-fail)
- Misunderstanding cleared by a text someone forgot to send.
- Love declared with no prior cost paid.
- Ambiguous walk-away with no genre-labeled tragedy intent.
- Sudden time-jump wedding with no earned choice shown.
- Fade on a kiss with unresolved wound still dominating.

### Ending QA (pass/fail)
- [ ] Could a listener retell the last 30 seconds as a decisive change?
- [ ] Does the close answer the question the cold open asked?
- [ ] Is HEA/HFN unmistakable?
- [ ] Would cutting the last 40 words destroy meaning? (If not, ending is soft.)

---

## Timeline presentation
- **Best:** Linear single-span (night, shift, road trip).
- **OK:** Aftermath reunion → brief flashback of break → present choice.
- **Risky:** Multi-month montage (reads as summary).

## Reveal mechanics
Love “reveals” are usually **emotional truth** (I was afraid / I lied about leaving), not whodunit. Plant the fear early; payoff by naming it and acting against it.
